About

I've spent the past 15+ years writing Windows software, mostly in C++ and Delphi, across a fairly wide range of domains — nuclear physics research, retail, finance, education, and PC utilities. At Samarkand State University, where I work as a programmer and scientific researcher, I build tools for gamma spectrometry and environmental radiation monitoring.

As a freelancer, I've built everything from a trading-platform extension and desktop security utilities to clinical assessment tools for therapists and a dictation grader for schools. I also build the occasional hobby tool for myself or to share for free, like a point-of-sale system for small businesses in Uzbekistan. I hold a Master's degree in the physics of nuclear and elementary particles from Samarkand State University.
